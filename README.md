# **Plushie Chaos Squad**

When the family leaves the house, everything seems perfectly normal.

The house is quiet. The lights are off. Nobody is watching.

But the plushies are awake.

While the family is away, the Plushie Chaos Squad secretly comes to life and gets to work. Their mission is simple: cause as much unhinged chaos as possible before the family returns.

A plushie might hide Mom's phone charger, rearrange Dad's tools, launch couch cushions into the neighbour's garden, build a pillow fortress in the living room, or empty the entire snack cupboard. Some plushies are sneaky, some are strong, some specialize in furniture-related destruction, and some are simply very good at making terrible decisions.

Of course, chaos requires organization.

The Plushie Chaos Squad therefore operates through a small Dispatch Center, which keeps track of all plushies, their abilities and energy levels, as well as the chaos incidents currently taking place. When a new chaos incident occurs, the Dispatch Center must find a suitable available plushie and send it into action.

Not every mission goes according to plan. Plushies can run out of chaos energy, already be busy with another incident, or simply fail spectacularly. When that happens, the squad has a collection of increasingly questionable backup plans.

The goal of the system is therefore to manage the squad and its chaos efficiently while also making sure that no plushie is accidentally assigned to two incidents at the same time.

Because the family may think their house is safe while they're away.

They are very, very wrong.
